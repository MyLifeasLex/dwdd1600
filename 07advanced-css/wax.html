Wax Treatment

How to Replace the Wax in a Lava Lamp

Step 1: Prepare the Replacement Wax
If using pre-made replacement wax, follow the manufacturer's instructions. If preparing your own wax, ensure it has been properly blended before heating.
Step 2: Melt the Wax
Place the wax in a heat-safe container and warm it until it becomes completely liquid. Avoid overheating the wax, as excessive heat can affect its performance.
Step 3: Reinstall the Heating Coil
Place the heating coil back into the bottom of the clean bottle. Make sure it sits flat against the bottom of the glass.
Step 4: Pour the Wax into the Bottle
Carefully pour the melted wax into the bottle. Pour slowly to minimize bubbles and splashing.
Step 5: Allow the Wax to Cool
Leave the bottle upright until the wax has completely solidified at the bottom. Do not move or shake the bottle while the wax is cooling.
Step 6: Continue the Restoration
Once the wax has fully cooled and hardened, the bottle is ready to be filled with the master fluid and any remaining restoration steps.

How to Restore Existing Lava Lamp Wax

Step 1: Inspect the Wax
Examine the wax for dirt, discoloration, debris, or signs of contamination. Remove any foreign material before proceeding.
Step 2: Melt the Wax
Place the wax in a clean, heat-safe container and warm it until it becomes completely liquid.
Step 3: Filter the Wax (Optional)
If debris is present, carefully pour the melted wax through a fine metal mesh strainer or other suitable filter into a clean container.
Step 4: Clean the Container
Remove any residue from the original melting container before returning the wax.
Step 5: Reinstall the Heating Coil
Place the heating coil into the bottom of the clean lava lamp bottle.
Step 6: Return the Wax to the Bottle
Slowly pour the cleaned, melted wax back into the bottle, taking care not to introduce unnecessary air bubbles.
Step 7: Allow the Wax to Solidify
Leave the bottle undisturbed until the wax has cooled completely and formed a solid layer at the bottom.
Step 8: Continue the Restoration
After the wax has hardened, proceed with refilling the bottle with master fluid and completing the remaining restoration process.

One small correction for technical accuracy: the heating coil should generally be placed in the bottle before pouring the wax only if it has been removed and you're able to position it correctly. In many restorations, the coil is actually lowered into the bottle after the wax has been poured and is still warm so it settles into the center of the wax puck. That method is more common among experienced restorers and helps ensure proper wax circulation.
